SteadKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

SteadKeep is built to keep your information private. The app has no user accounts, no ads, and no third-party tracking. The developer runs no servers and never collects, receives, or has access to your personal data. Your habits and progress stay on your device and — if you use iCloud — sync privately through your own iCloud account.

Information we collect

None. SteadKeep does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any server operated by the developer. There is no sign-up, no login, and no analytics or usage tracking of any kind.

Where your data lives

The habits, tasks, notes, streaks, points, and settings you create are stored locally on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, this data also syncs across your own Apple devices through Apple’s iCloud service (CloudKit), stored in your private iCloud database. This sync is provided and secured by Apple; the developer cannot see or access the contents of your iCloud data. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Photos

If you choose a custom photo as a habit icon, you select it through Apple’s built-in photo picker. SteadKeep does not request access to your photo library — only the single image you pick is imported. That image is stored with your habit data on your device and in your private iCloud; it is never uploaded anywhere else.

Notifications

SteadKeep can send reminders and other alerts. These are local notifications scheduled and generated entirely on your device. No notification content is sent to or through the developer or any third party.

Purchases

SteadKeep offers an optional Premium upgrade as an in-app purchase. All payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your name, card number, or any payment details — only Apple’s confirmation that a purchase is active.

No third-party tracking or advertising

SteadKeep contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking libraries. It does not track you across other apps or websites and does not share data with data brokers.

Children

SteadKeep does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it is suitable for users of all ages.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data remains available until you remove it. Deleting a habit or task removes it from the app. Deleting SteadKeep removes the data stored on that device. To remove data synced to iCloud, delete it within the app on a signed-in device, or manage SteadKeep’s iCloud storage in the iOS Settings app under your Apple Account → iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of SteadKeep after an update means you accept the revised policy.
