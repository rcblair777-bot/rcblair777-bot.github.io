Getting started

SteadKeep has three tabs along the bottom:

  • Today — everything scheduled for today. Tap a habit to mark it complete; tap again to undo.
  • Progress — an at-a-glance overview of how you're doing. Premium unlocks full stats: the completion-history graph, calendar, and per-habit breakdown.
  • Manage — add, edit, reorder (drag the handle), or delete habits and tasks.

The gear icon opens Settings, where you'll find your personality, theme, notification options, Premium, and this guide.

Habits

Habits are recurring things you want to keep doing. When you create one, you choose:

How often

Daily, weekdays, weekends, weekly, monthly, or yearly.

Strict vs flexible

A strict habit is due on a specific day — every Monday, the 15th of the month, a set date each year. A flexible habit is due anytime within its period — "once a week," on whatever day suits you. Flexible habits appear on Today during a sensible window and won't break your streak just because you didn't do them on a particular day.

Targets (more than once per period)

A habit can ask for several completions per period — "drink water 6 times a day," "run 3 times this week." It's only marked done once you reach the target, and the widget/Today view show your progress toward it (e.g. "1/3 this week").

Icon, color & reminder

Pick an icon from the full symbol grid and any color — the swatches or the color wheel (all free) — and optionally set a reminder time (see Reminders). Using your own photo from your library as the icon is a Premium feature.

Tasks

Tasks are one-time to-dos, not recurring habits. They stay on your Today list until you complete them. A task can have a due date and an optional reminder. Due-dated tasks also appear a day early under a "Tomorrow" heading so nothing sneaks up on you.

Completing & editing

Tap a habit or task to complete it; tap again to undo. To keep your history honest, you can only mark or unmark a habit for today or yesterday — SteadKeep intentionally doesn't let you rewrite weeks of past days. Points are only awarded for completions made on the actual day.

Streaks

A streak is the number of consecutive periods you kept a habit. For a daily habit that's consecutive days; for weekly/monthly/yearly it's consecutive weeks/months/years. Missing a period the habit was genuinely due breaks the streak. Flexible habits only break if you miss the entire period, and paused days never count against you. Your longest streak is saved even after a streak ends.

Pause Mode

Going on holiday or taking a deliberate break? Pause a habit for a date range. Paused days are neutral — they don't count as missed, so your streak is protected — and reminders stop until the pause ends. A day you did complete before pausing still counts. When the pause ends, the habit and its reminders simply resume.

Points & Grace Days

You earn points as you build habits:

  • +1 for each habit completion
  • +3 when you finish everything due in a day
  • +2 for keeping your overall streak alive
  • +10 when you hit a streak milestone (7, 30, 100 days, and beyond)

You can hold up to 100 points. Spend 25 points on a Grace Day (Premium), which protects a streak on a day you missed — you can bank up to 3 at a time. Grace Days are the safety net for the occasional off day without losing weeks of progress.

Reminders & notifications

Give a habit a reminder time and SteadKeep will nudge you, with a gentle follow-up a bit later. Once you complete a habit, its reminders stop for the rest of that period — you won't be nagged about something you've already done. There are a few kinds of notifications:

  • Habit reminders (free) — your chosen time, plus a follow-up.
  • Streak Risk (free) — a last-chance evening alert if something you're truly due to do is still unfinished.
  • Morning Motivation and Completion Celebration (Premium) — an optional morning kick-off and a well-done message when you finish everything.

Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they work even if the app is closed — just make sure notifications are allowed for SteadKeep in the iOS Settings app.

Personalities, themes & widgets

Personalities

Choose the voice SteadKeep uses to encourage you: Coach, Best Friend, Sarcastic Robot, Drill Sergeant, Dracula, or Surfer Dude. Coach is free; Premium unlocks the other five.

Themes & appearance

Pick an app theme and switch between light and dark mode in Settings. Premium adds the full range of color themes.

Home Screen widget

Add the SteadKeep widget for an at-a-glance view of your day. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the icons jiggle, tap the + in the corner, search for SteadKeep, and add the small or medium size. It shows your completion count, what's up next, and your streak.

Premium

SteadKeep is free to use with up to 3 habits. Premium is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks:

  • Unlimited habits
  • All six personalities
  • Grace Days
  • Full stats — the completion-history graph, calendar, and per-habit breakdown
  • Every color theme
  • Photo habit icons (use your own photo from your library)
  • Morning Motivation & Completion Celebration notifications

Buy it from the Premium screen in Settings. If you get a new device or reinstall, tap Restore Purchases (on the Premium screen) while signed in with the same Apple Account and it comes right back.

Your data & privacy

Your habits, tasks, and progress are stored on your device and sync privately through your own iCloud account, so they're available on all your devices. There are no accounts to create, no ads, and no third-party tracking. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is SteadKeep free?

Yes — you can track up to 3 habits for free. Premium is a one-time $2.99 purchase that removes the limit and unlocks the extras above.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It's a single one-time purchase — you pay once and keep it.

I got a new phone. How do I get Premium back?

On the Premium screen in Settings, tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does my data sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. As long as both devices are signed into the same iCloud account with iCloud enabled, SteadKeep syncs automatically. Sync can take a few moments over a slow connection.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your device and in your own private iCloud. The developer runs no servers and can't see your data, and there's no tracking or advertising.

Why can I only edit today and yesterday?

To keep your streaks and history honest. SteadKeep deliberately doesn't let you rewrite older days.

What's the difference between a strict and a flexible habit?

Strict habits are due on a specific day (e.g. every Monday). Flexible habits are due anytime within the period (e.g. once a week, your choice of day) and only break your streak if you miss the whole period.

Can I do a habit more than once a day (or week)?

Yes — set a target when creating or editing the habit (e.g. "6 times a day"). It's marked done once you reach the target, and you'll see your progress toward it.

What happens to my streak if I pause a habit?

Nothing bad — paused days are neutral and never count as missed. Anything you completed before pausing still counts, and the habit resumes when the pause ends.

How do Grace Days work?

A Grace Day protects a streak on a day you missed. Earn points by completing habits, then spend 25 points on a Grace Day (you can hold up to 3). Grace Days require Premium.

I completed a habit but still got a reminder — why?

Completing a habit stops its reminders for the rest of that period, so this shouldn't happen on the current version. Make sure SteadKeep is updated to the latest version; if it keeps happening, please email us with the details.

Can I use my own photo as a habit icon?

That's a Premium feature. Every account gets the full symbol grid and every color for free; using a photo from your library as a habit icon comes with Premium. With Premium, tap the photo option when choosing an icon and pick an image — only the photo you select is imported.

Do reminders work when the app is closed?

Yes — reminders are scheduled on your device. Just make sure notifications are turned on for SteadKeep in the iOS Settings app.

How do I add the Home Screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen until the icons jiggle, tap the + in the corner, search for SteadKeep, and add the small or medium widget.
